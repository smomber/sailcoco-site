What SailCoCo does

Every country puts your boat, your crew, and your gear under its own rules. SailCoCo turns that maze into a plan — researched by our AI pipeline, corroborated across official sources, and continuously monitored for changes.

🧭 Trip Compliance

Plan a multi-country route and get each stop's entry, visa, and clearance requirements — every fact carrying its source and last-verified date.

💬 CoCo Agent

An AI assistant that knows your trip and your paperwork. Ask anything; answers cite sources and dates. When it can't verify, it says so and investigates — it never guesses. Informational only, not legal advice.

🛃 Import Clock & Ship's Papers

Countdown timers on cruising permits and temporary imports, renewal steps with fees, and one organized home for vessel documents — registration, insurance, radio license, clearance papers.

🌿 Biosecurity, Pets & Drones

What you must dispose, declare, or can keep at each border; pet import timelines and certificates; whether your drone can fly, needs a permit, or should stay stowed.

📣 Live Community Feed

Real-time official notices for the countries on your route, plus corroborated reports from cruisers on the ground — fee changes, office hours, inspection wait times.

⚓ Helm Display Panel

Your compliance instruments on the boat's own screens via Signal K — permit clocks and arrival status next to speed and depth. The NOW zone is free for every boat, forever.

Who it's for: cruising sailors and liveaboards crossing borders on their own boats; guests and crew joining someone else's boat; delivery skippers and professional crew (our Pro tier); and charter operators running trips for others.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

The short version of what our Terms of Service provide:

Cancelling a subscription

Cancel anytime with one click in your account settings (or by email to support@sailcoco.com) — no phone call, no retention hoops. Cancellation stops future charges and takes effect at the end of your current billing period; you keep access until then. Amounts already charged for the current period are not refunded except where the law requires.

Renewals & reminders

Annual plans renew once a year. We email a renewal reminder 15–45 days before each renewal, and a separate reminder 3–21 days before an introductory price steps up to the standard price.

One-time purchases

Founding Crew and Passage Pack do not auto-renew and no card is kept on file for them. A Passage Pack is not refundable once you create your route or its access window begins. If SailCoCo is discontinued within 24 months of a Founding Crew purchase, a pro-rata refund applies per our Terms.

App store purchases & EU/UK customers

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play follow those stores' refund policies and processes. Consumers in the EEA and the United Kingdom have a 14-day right of withdrawal for digital services as described in our Terms.
