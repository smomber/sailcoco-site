What SailCoCo does

Every country puts your boat, your crew, and your gear under its own rules. SailCoCo turns that maze into a plan — researched by our AI pipeline and shown with its sources, each with the date that source was last checked.

🧭 Trip Compliance

Plan a multi-country route and see each stop's entry, visa, and clearance requirements — each fact showing its source and the date that source was last checked.

💬 CoCo Agent

An AI assistant that knows your trip and your paperwork. Ask about your route; answers cite sources and dates. When it cannot find a source, it says so and investigates. Informational only, not legal advice.

🛃 Import Clock & Ship's Papers

Countdown timers on cruising permits and temporary imports, renewal steps with fees, and one organized home for vessel documents — registration, insurance, radio license, clearance papers.

🌿 Biosecurity, Pets & Drones

What you must dispose, declare, or can keep at each border; pet import timelines and certificates; whether your drone can fly, needs a permit, or should stay stowed.

📣 Live Community Feed

Official notices for the countries on your route, plus reports from cruisers on the ground — fee changes, office hours, inspection wait times.

⚓ Helm Display Panel

Your compliance instruments on the boat's own screens via Signal K — permit clocks and arrival status next to speed and depth.

Who it's for: cruising sailors and liveaboards crossing borders on their own boats; guests and crew joining someone else's boat; delivery skippers and paid crew; and charter operators running trips for others.
