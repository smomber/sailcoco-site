Hot / Cold CoCo

One tap between connected and conserving.

Offshore, battery and signal are the same problem. Hot CoCo is live. Cold CoCo runs from your last sync and stops spending power on anything that needs a connection. The switch is one tap from the home screen, from Account, or from the badge in the corner of every screen.

Hot CoCo

Default · live

Connected and researching. The agent verifies against live sources, every screen shows current data, and chat is open in-app, over WhatsApp/SMS, and in Live Translate.

Cold CoCo

Underway

Deliberately conserving. Live chat, scanning and Live Translate pause; chat falls back to WhatsApp/SMS, which only needs cell signal. Screens keep working from whatever was already synced.
