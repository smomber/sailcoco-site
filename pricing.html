Skipper introductory rates are a first-term discount. Renewals bill at the standard rate shown beside them.

Why annual only?

Cruising seasons are annual, and compliance research is a standing cost per country whether you sail in March or October. One rate, one renewal.

What is a country?

One nation added to your trip, with its full compliance entry unlocked. Swap countries as your plans change; the count is how many you hold at once.

What happens on renewal?

You pay the standard annual price. The intro figure applies to your first year only — it's a one-time discount, not a lower ongoing rate.

Do passengers pay?

Not if they're on your Skipper bundle. Berths are invited free and no card is needed to accept.
